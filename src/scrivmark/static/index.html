Working in scrivmark

  • Read the manuscript as rendered prose. Changes show as deleted and inserted text.
  • Comment: select some text, then press Comment.
  • Edit: hover over a paragraph and press ✎ to edit its Markdown in place.
  • Ask your agent: select text and tell Claude or Codex “look at my selection”, or press Ask agent to queue a question.
  • Authors click a change to accept or reject it, and reply to or resolve comments in the margin.
  • Keys: j/k next and previous item.
