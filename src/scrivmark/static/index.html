Working in scrivmark

  • Read the manuscript as rendered prose. Changes show as deleted and inserted text.
  • Comment: select some text, then press Comment.
  • Edit: hover over a paragraph and press ✎ to edit its Markdown in place.
  • Ask your agent: select text and tell Claude or Codex “look at my selection”, or press Ask agent. Asks stay in the margin, in blue, only for you; tell your agent “check my asks” whenever you like, and its answers appear on the cards. Comment from this turns an answer into a comment for the author.
  • Authors click a change (or Tab to it and press Enter) to accept or reject it. A decision can be undone from the message that follows, or with scrivmark undo.
  • Keys: j/k move through everything still unresolved, including changes outside the running text.
